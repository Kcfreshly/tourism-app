# Owner handoff checklist

## To start coding today

- Extract the complete package into or alongside the chosen repository.
- Give the agent `prompts/01_MASTER_BUILD_PROMPT.md` and access to the package.
- Use demo mode and the fictional fixtures; no keys required for the first vertical slice.
- Confirm or replace the proposed Lagos-first assumption and working product name.
- Keep one issue list for owner decisions; do not let unresolved branding stop domain implementation.

## To replace demo with live supply

- Fill supplier verification template; secure rate/capacity agreement and permitted photos.
- Configure vehicle/guide capacity, service zones, hours, travel buffers and replacement process.
- Approve line-item inclusions, fees, tax treatment and cancellation/refund policies.
- Enter real personnel profiles with consent and verification; no personal IDs in public assets.
- Configure actual payment, model, notifications and storage providers with server-only secrets.
- Verify support staffing, contacts and escalation; do not claim 24/7 unless staffed.
- Complete sandbox webhook/race/refund tests and an on-ground trip rehearsal.
- Review backups, monitoring, retention and incident ownership.
- Enable live checkout only after the gates in docs/06 pass.

## Review the coding agent's delivery

Ask it to demonstrate the entire journey on mobile and desktop, a failed checkout, a last-seat race, a pending driver assignment, a support acknowledgement and a paid-plan change. Require exact test results and a list of remaining mock adapters. Screenshots alone do not show booking reliability.
